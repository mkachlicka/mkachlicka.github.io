Research internship in sleep research with intracranial EEG (iEEG), University of Bern

Posted: May 5, 2026

Opportunity

We are offering a research internship for a motivated student interested in sleep, brain activity, and data analysis. The project will investigate sleep patterns using intracranial EEG (iEEG) recordings, which are high temporal and spatial-resolution data invasively recorded from the human brain. The internship is unpaid but can be credited as an internship in Psychology. If interested, the internship can subsequently be extended to include a thesis. For more information, please download: 'CCN iEEG Internship 2026'. ***This position has been filled, thank you for your applications!
